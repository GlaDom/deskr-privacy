deskr Privacy Policy

Your data stays on your device

Last updated — May 10, 2026

TL;DR

Deskr stores everything locally on your phone. We don't collect, transmit, or sell any personal data. There are no accounts, no analytics, and no tracking. Your work-hour data never leaves your device.

What Deskr Does

Deskr is a home-office hour tracker that helps you monitor how many hours you work from home versus the office and whether you're within your monthly budget. All data is stored in a local database on your device.

Data We Collect

None. Deskr does not collect, store, or process any data on external servers. Specifically:

  • No personal information is transmitted off your device
  • No analytics or crash reporting SDKs are included
  • No advertising frameworks or tracking pixels are used
  • No cookies or browser fingerprinting is employed
  • No user accounts or authentication systems exist

Data Stored on Your Device

The following data is stored locally on your phone and never shared:

  • Your name (as entered during onboarding)
  • Your weekly contracted hours and home-office percentage
  • Daily work entries (date, type, hours)

This data is stored in a local SQLite database. If you uninstall the app, all data is permanently deleted.

Data Sharing

Deskr does not share any data with third parties. There are no partnerships, integrations, or data-sharing agreements of any kind. The optional CSV export feature saves a file to your device — the destination of that file is entirely under your control.

Third-Party Services

Deskr does not integrate with any third-party services, APIs, or SDKs. The app functions entirely offline after installation.

Children's Privacy

Deskr is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from children. Since no data is collected from any user, this is inherently satisfied.

Your Rights

Since all data is stored locally on your device, you have complete control over it at all times. You can:

  • View all your data directly in the app
  • Export your data as a CSV file from Settings
  • Delete all data from within the app's Settings screen
  • Uninstall the app to permanently remove all data

Changes to This Policy

If Deskr ever introduces features that affect data handling (such as cloud sync or backups), this policy will be updated before those features are released. The "last updated" date at the top will always reflect the most recent revision.
